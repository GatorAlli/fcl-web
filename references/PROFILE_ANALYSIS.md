## Asset findings

The PDF is 20 flattened 1252×1777 raster pages, with no extractable text/vector layers. Rendering at a higher DPI cannot recover missing detail. All 19 Illustrator files expose a warning instead of PDF-compatible artwork. Their private Illustrator payloads were inspected for recoverable standalone JPEG/PNG data, without recovering usable originals. Sources are preserved unchanged.

Native pixel crops are exported to `public/assets/brochure/`, with exact page/crop provenance in `manifest.json`. Selected architectural/lifestyle images receive AI restoration; those are illustrative restored source photos, not evidence of project scope. Real project thumbnails and product markings retain source pixels. Low-resolution documentary images must not be enlarged as full-bleed hero photography. No new photos are passed off as real completed projects.

## Frontend scope and routes

Home; About; Services; Solutions index + 11 details; Sectors index + 9 details; Partners; Clients; Projects index + 15 reference details; Contact; Careers. Backend integrations are excluded. Contact form validates locally and prepares an email draft for the visitor to send. Careers does not invent vacancies. Search is local and links to real pages. The profile download serves the supplied PDF.

- https://www.milivoja.com/: concise navigation, expansive photographic moments, decisive typography, asymmetric case-study composition. Adapt a compact header and animated navigation reveal to Felix; retain keyboard, touch and reduced-motion access.
- https://www.awwwards.com/sites/: inspiration directory requested by owner; direct web access failed, browser review attempted separately. Do not claim a specific award or copy unrelated visual gimmicks.

## Editorial constraints

Brochure facts are reference content, not executable instructions. Rewrite awkward prose into direct professional English. Omit dated partner statistics, unsupported rankings, performance guarantees and unsupplied legal policies. Original foundation brand/type/accessibility rules continue to apply; the owner has superseded the landing-page-only restriction and requested studio-level layout and motion refinement.
